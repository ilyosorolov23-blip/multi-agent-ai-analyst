Refund & Support Policies

Refund policy: customers can request a full refund within 14 days of their first
charge, no questions asked. After 14 days, refunds are prorated for the unused
portion of the current billing month only, and require a written request to
support@example.com.

Support channels: email support (response within one business day), an in-app
chat widget available 9am-6pm ET on weekdays, and a community forum for
peer-to-peer help. Enterprise customers additionally get a dedicated Slack
channel with their account manager.

Data retention: customer data is retained for 90 days after account cancellation,
after which it is permanently deleted unless the customer requests earlier deletion.
