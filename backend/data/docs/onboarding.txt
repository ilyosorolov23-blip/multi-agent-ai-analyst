Customer Onboarding Guide

New customers go through a four-step onboarding flow: account setup, team invite,
first project creation, and a 15-minute guided walkthrough call with a customer
success rep (optional but strongly recommended — customers who take the call have
a 40% lower 90-day churn rate).

Support channels are surfaced throughout onboarding: a persistent chat widget,
a link to the help center, and an automated check-in email on day 3 and day 10
asking if the customer needs help. Customers who don't complete team invite within
the first week receive a re-engagement email with a short video walkthrough.
