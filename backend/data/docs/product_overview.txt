Product Overview

Our company sells three products:

1. Orbit — a project management tool for small teams, priced at $12/user/month.
2. Pulse — a customer analytics dashboard that tracks usage and churn signals,
   priced at $29/user/month.
3. Beacon — an automated email and SMS marketing tool for e-commerce stores,
   priced at $19/user/month.

All three products share a single sign-on and a unified billing account. Customers
typically start on Orbit and expand into Pulse once their team grows past ten people.

Common churn reasons reported by the support team: (1) the customer's team shrank
and the tool no longer fit their workflow, (2) they found a competitor with a lower
price point, (3) they never fully onboarded and stopped seeing value within the
first 30 days.
